Why this exists

Proof that the people who love this game are still here, and still building.

Destiny's future gets decided in rooms we are not in. This board is the argument for letting us in — a public, organised body of work showing that the community is alive, thinking hard about the game's problems, and capable of proposing real answers rather than complaints.

The point is not to hand Bungie a wishlist. It is to do the work: name the problem, show the direction, and put it somewhere it can be read. If enough of that accumulates in one place, it stops being noise on a timeline and starts being something a studio can actually look at.

AI is part of how we get there. A person with an idea and no studio behind them can now render the destination, cut the trailer, mock the HUD, and prototype the encounter. That collapses the distance between having an idea and showing it — which is exactly the distance that used to keep most of this community out of the conversation.

Our principles

Share openly

Ideas grow when they are shared. Bring your concepts, explorations, references and questions into the open where everyone can see them.

Build together

The best ideas are collaborative. Challenge, expand and refine each other's work to push it further than any one person can alone.

Keep the thread connected

Context matters. Cite what inspired you so the community can follow an idea back to where it started and forward to where it went.

No house vision

Ideas don't have to agree.

This is not one definitive community design document. One contributor may want the Traveler gone entirely; another may build a concept around its return. One may redesign the subclasses; another may ask whether classes should exist at all.

Both belong here, linked as alternatives. Concepts can support, challenge, remix or directly contradict one another. Nothing on this board is canon — the collection of ideas is the vision.
