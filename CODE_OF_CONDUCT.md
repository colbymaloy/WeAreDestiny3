# Code of Conduct

This project only works if people are willing to post unfinished ideas in public. Protect that.

## The short version

**Critique the work, not the person.** "This HUD hides the thing I need mid-fight" is useful. "This is garbage" is not, and neither is anything about who made it.

**Nobody owes you a medium.** Traditional artists, 3D artists, designers, developers, musicians, writers and people generating with AI are all equally welcome here. Arguments about whether someone's tools count are off-topic — take them elsewhere.

**Credit people.** Building on someone else's concept is the point of this project. Name them and link them when you do.

**Feedback is a request, not a demand.** Creators decide what happens to their own work.

## Not welcome

Harassment, personal attacks, or sustained disruption. Demeaning or discriminatory comments of any kind, including about someone's identity, background or skill level. Sexual content or unwanted sexual attention. Publishing someone's private information. Posting work that isn't yours as if it were. Presenting anything here as genuine or leaked Destiny material.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, and anywhere else someone is representing this project.

## Reporting

Open an issue if it's something the community should see resolved in the open, or contact the maintainer directly at **colbymaloydev@gmail.com** if it isn't. Reports are handled privately.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may block accounts. The response fits the behaviour: usually a warning, sometimes an immediate removal.
